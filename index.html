How to play

Use this tracker for Horse Race style darts. Each player gets a numbered horse. Every hit moves their horse down the lane.

  • Assign unique horses numbered 1–10.
  • Tap Hit +1 when that horse is scored.
  • Use Undo for corrections.
  • Advance turns manually or just track the race live.
  • First horse to the finish wins the race.
